# Masked Sobolev Training for Feasibility-Reliable Optimization Proxies

**NeurIPS 2026** · Andrew W. Rosemberg, Joaquim Dias Garcia, Russell Bent, and Pascal Van Hentenryck

[Visit the paper website](https://andrewrosemberg.github.io/NeurIPS2026-Masked-Sobolev-Optimization-Proxies/)

The website presents masked Sobolev training for optimization proxies, examining
how local sensitivity information supports generalization in objective quality
and feasibility. It introduces the method, illustrates its behavior in Acrobot
control, AC optimal power flow, and mean–variance portfolio optimization, and
summarizes the paper’s theoretical perspective and experimental findings.
